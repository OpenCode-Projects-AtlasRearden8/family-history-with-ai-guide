## Pre-publication privacy pass

- [ ] Read the whole manuscript asking: "Is a living person named, dated,
      addressed, or diagnosed here?"
- [ ] Checked photos and captions for the same.
- [ ] Every "FAMILY COPY" mark placed where extras exist.
- [ ] Privacy log up to date; changes-of-mind honored.
- [ ] No addresses, emails, phone numbers, full birth dates of the living,
      medical or legal details anywhere in the shared version.
