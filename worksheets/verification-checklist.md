# Verification Checklist

*Print and tape near your keyboard. Run it before any claim enters a chapter.
(The full routine: the Verification chapter.)*

## Before writing a fact into the book

- [ ] 1. The claim is written in my source log with its proposer named
      (relative, record, or AI — and "AI" claims stay UNVERIFIED until proven).
- [ ] 2. I named the record type that could prove it (census, certificate,
      manifest, letter…).
- [ ] 3. I actually looked at the record or the original document — not just
      someone's summary, index entry, or an AI description of it.
- [ ] 4. The specific detail matches (exact date, spelling, ship, address),
      not just something nearby in the same record.
- [ ] 5. The source is logged: title, repository, date I checked, copy saved
      into `family-history/originals/` or `documents/`.
- [ ] 6. If sources disagree, I noted the conflict in the book rather than
      silently choosing one.
- [ ] 7. If the claim could NOT be verified: it's cut, or written with its
      doubt attached ("Maya says 1948; city license records show 1951").

## AI-output quick screen

- [ ] The AI gave me a *source I can open* (not "typically," "usually," or a
      plausible-sounding citation I can't find).
- [ ] For anything the AI transcribed (handwriting, census, letters), I
      checked the transcription against the original image at full zoom.
- [ ] No AI-supplied name for an unidentified photo went into a caption.

## Ten-minute audit (do monthly)

- [ ] Open the source log; find rows where proposer was AI.
- [ ] Check each is VERIFIED or CUT — no UNVERIFIED facts remain in drafts.
- [ ] Skim the latest chapter draft for any claim without a cited source.
- [ ] Skim captions for names no source identified.
